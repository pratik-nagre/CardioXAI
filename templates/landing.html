Core Contributions

What Makes This System Unique

Bridging the gap between black-box ML predictions and clinically actionable, explainable patient pathways.

🧠

Latent Space Optimization

Uses a Variational Autoencoder to project patients into a smooth latent manifold, ensuring counterfactuals are physiologically plausible rather than arbitrary feature perturbations.

⚖️

Feasibility Constraints

Counterfactual generation respects immutable features (sex, prior events) and applies directional constraints (age cannot decrease), ensuring medical realism in all interventions.

📊

Ensemble Risk Scoring

Combines Cox Proportional Hazards survival analysis with XGBoost, LightGBM, and Random Forest classifiers to produce robust, consensus-driven risk estimates.

🔬

Explainability (XAI)

Feature-level Cox coefficient contributions are visualized for every patient, showing exactly which clinical factors drive risk up or protect against cardiovascular disease.

💊

Clinical Prescription

The system generates step-by-step intervention plans — e.g., target BMI to 26.5, reduce cigarettes to 0 — providing physicians with concrete, prioritized action items.

📈

Survival Trajectory

Kaplan-Meier-style survival curves for both baseline and optimized patient profiles allow visual comparison of risk reduction over 1, 3, 5, and 10-year horizons.

Technical Pipeline

How It Works

A four-stage pipeline from raw patient data to actionable clinical recourse.

01

Data Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

The Framingham Heart Study Cohort (PERIOD=1 baseline) is stratified-split to prevent leakage. Continuous features are scaled with StandardScaler, categorical education is one-hot encoded, and missingness is imputed using train-set statistics.

Framingham Dataset StandardScaler Train/Test Split Median Imputation
02

Ensemble Risk Model Training

A Cox Proportional Hazards model provides survival analysis and interpretable log-hazard coefficients. XGBoost, LightGBM, and Random Forest classifiers provide complementary probabilistic risk estimates, forming an ensemble voting system.

Cox PHM (lifelines) XGBoost LightGBM Random Forest
03

Variational Autoencoder (VAE) Manifold Learning

A deep VAE is trained on the patient feature space to learn a smooth, low-dimensional latent representation. This ensures that optimized counterfactual patients remain "on-manifold" and physiologically plausible.

PyTorch VAE Latent Space Reconstruction Loss KL Divergence
04

Feasibility-Guided Counterfactual Optimization

The optimizer performs gradient-based search in latent space to minimize the ensemble risk score, subject to constraints: immutable features are frozen, directional features are bounded, and the result is decoded back to native feature space as a clinical prescription.
